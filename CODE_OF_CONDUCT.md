# Code of Conduct

Navet Cards welcomes users and contributors of different backgrounds and experience levels. Be respectful, ask questions in good faith, and keep feedback focused on behavior, evidence, and actionable improvements.

Harassment, threats, discrimination, personal attacks, publication of private information, and repeated disruption are unacceptable. These standards apply to issues, pull requests, Discussions, and other project spaces.

Report concerns privately to `security@navet.app`, including what happened and relevant links. Maintainers may remove comments, close conversations, reject contributions, or block participants based on the severity and pattern of the behavior.
